Common questions

What should I upload?

A clear image of a blank garment and a separate image of your graphic work best.

Will the graphic be reproduced exactly?

Not necessarily. Image generation may alter small text, edges, colors, proportions, and placement. Use an image editor to composite original artwork for precise presentation and confirm final production files with your manufacturer.

Does Garment Lab store my images?

The developer does not operate a separate backend that independently stores uploads. ChatGPT/OpenAI processing and data handling are governed by their current policies and settings; see the privacy policy.
